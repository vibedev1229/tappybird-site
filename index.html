Tappy Bird

Tap to fly. Dodge the pipes. Beat your best.

Free on iPhone (iOS 16 and later) and Android 6.0 and later

One tap. That is the whole control scheme.

The bird falls, every tap flaps it higher, and the only thing between you and a new best score is an endless run of gap pipes. Simple to start, hard to put down. It plays fully offline, needs no account, and every bird can be earned by playing.

What is in it

  • Two complete art stylesA smooth Modern look and a retro Pixel mode, switchable any time in Settings.
  • Nine bird skinsClassic, Parrot, Penguin, Eagle, Robot, Ninja, Dragon, Rainbow and Phoenix, unlocked with coins you earn.
  • Skill fair pipesEvery gap is placed somewhere the bird can actually reach, so a run never ends on an impossible spawn.
  • Game CenterA global leaderboard and five achievements at scores of 1, 10, 25, 50 and 100.
  • Plays offlineNo sign up, no login, no personal details. Your progress stays on your device.
  • Optional purchasesRemove Ads and a Premium Pack if you want them. Nothing is locked behind them.
